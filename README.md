✨ What is RigVeda Odyssey?
RigVeda Odyssey is a beautifully crafted, fully accessible web application bringing the wisdom, stories, and wonders of the Rigveda to life. Explore Vedic hymns, discover legendary deities, and interact with ancient knowledge through modern UI/UX design, advanced audio integration, and playful gamification.

Mission:

Bridge the gap between India’s spiritual literature and digital technology.

Make Rigveda knowledge joyful and interactive for all, with no barriers.

📚 Key Features
Explore Mandalas (1-10): Browse and listen to authentic hymns, access curated transcripts, and visualize their meaning.

Deity Collector: Unlock, view, and interact with 24 Rigvedic gods, complete with lore, art, NFT-style cards, and achievements.

Podcast Hub: Listen to multi-mandala podcast series with full transcripts (en/sanskrit), indexed for easy discovery.

Mascot “Rishi”: Meet your playful AI guide for introductions, feedback, and daily insights.

Story Mode: Play through animated Vedic adventures—choose pathways, make decisions, reveal spiritual lessons.

Games Hub: Test your wit and reflexes with themed mini-games (Indra’s Lightning Leap and more!).

Achievement System: Collect badges, track progress, and unlock secret awards.

Advanced Audio System: Context-aware background music, rich sound effects, playback controls (compliant with browser autoplay policies).

Interactive UI: Particle backgrounds, glassmorphism panels, Swiss design principles, clickable suggestions, typewriter effects, and responsive layouts.

Global Search: Instantly find hymns, deities, podcasts, topics, and more.

Mobile & Desktop Friendly: Optimized for all major browsers and device types.

🔒 No Login, No Paywall
Public demo link: Free, instant access—no registration.

**All code/assets (except large audio files) tracked for transparency, compliance, and performance.

🛠️ Tech Stack
Frontend: React.js, JavaScript, Tailwind CSS, D3.js, Framer Motion, Lucide React

Hosting: Vercel or Netlify

CDN: Cloudinary (audio files, images), or GitHub Releases for large assets

📂 Project Structure
Follows modern React conventions:

public/ (media, data, images, audio, transcripts, podcasts)

src/components/ (interactive features, loading, navigation, game mechanics)

src/context/ (state + audio managers)

src/data/ (deities, hymns, topics, achievements, facts, metadata)

.gitignore (tracks only essential files, ignores bulk audio except critical UX assets)

README.md (project overview, setup instructions, attribution, licensing)
